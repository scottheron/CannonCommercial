Estate Goals

Cannon Commercial was founded in 1992 as a property management firm and has since evolved into a transaction-based brokerage of commercial properties throughout Seattle and the Greater Puget Sound area. We offer sales and leasing services to both users and investors alike.


Though purposefully small in size, Cannon Commercial maintains a razor-sharp focus on market conditions and trends, taking full advantage of the best available technical tools and resources. Our many years of experience, coupled with a reputation for integrity, allow us quick and easy access into all sectors of Seattle’s commercial real estate community.
